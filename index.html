Fleet 919 is a North America based Star Citizen org with members spread across timezones. We make room for all play styles — whether that's Industrial, FPS, Pilots, or any other game loop Star Citizen has to offer.

No time-in-game requirements. Discord is required and voice chat is preferred. We're open to new players and veterans alike who are looking for a down to earth gaming community that understands real life takes precedence.

Fleet 919 exists because of its membership. Every action should strenghten the community.

MEMBERS WILL:

  • Support one another in all endeavors.
  • Uphold the highest standards of conduct and professionalism.
  • Contribute to the growth and success of the fleet.
  • Remember that no mission succeeds alone.

Respect is non-negotiable.

MEMBERS WILL:

  • Speak professionally during operations
  • Resolve disagreements constructively and maturely.
  • Show patience toward newer players learning the game.

MEMBERS WILL NOT:

  • Harass, insult, or belittle other members (unless it's about ArcSolo's ears).
  • Create drama or division within the fleet.
  • Discriminate any member based on skill, nationality, or other characteristics.
  • Use fleet communication channels for personal attacks or harassment.

When wearing the Fleet 919 name, you represent the fleet and its values.

MEMBERS WILL:

  • Conduct themselves with integrity and respect at all times.
  • Avoid toxic behavior or language toward another organization.
  • Respect allies and neutral parties.
  • Avoid any behavior that could damage the reputation of the fleet.

Problems are solved through communication.

MEMBERS WILL:

  • Address concerns immediately with leadership. Do not wait, hoping it'll get better.
  • Offer solutions and be willing to compromise.
  • Provide honest feedback. It's how we grow and become better as a group.
  • Avoid gossip and spreading rumors. If you hear something, say something to leadership.

OTHER TENANTS AND GUIDELINES

  • Avoid "Hot-micing" during events. No one wants to hear you sneeze or scold your cat for being on your desk.
  • New players are a protected class and should be treated with patience and understanding. Teach without arrogance, share freely, train the inexperienced, and lead by example.
  • Honor commitments and follow through on promises made to the fleet. We know life comes first, and we will never admonish someone for having a life outside the fleet. However, be considerate to your fellow members' time.
  • Don't steal, be a jackass, harass or abuse others and above all, NEVER EXPLOIT.

SIMUL CONSTRUCTUM

We are NOT built by ships.

We are NOT built by credits.

We are NOT built by loot.

We are built by the people who choose to stand together.
